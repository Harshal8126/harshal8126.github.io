Sonar breathing test

Plays a near-silent high tone and listens to its echo from your chest. Breathing moves the chest and changes the echo. No camera, no contact.

  • Lie or sit still in a quiet room with the phone 30-50 cm from your chest, resting on something solid, with the bottom edge (speaker and mic) pointing at you.
  • Do not talk. Keep nothing moving near the phone, such as a fan.
  • To check accuracy, pick a reference pace and follow the orb, or breathe at a steady count.
  • Some children, pets and a few adults can hear 17-18 kHz. Use 19-20 kHz and keep the level low.
