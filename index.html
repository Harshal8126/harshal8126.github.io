Sonar lock test

Calibrates on your fingertip pulse, then follows your breathing from beside the bed using the soft sound it plays. Heart rate from sonar is experimental. Nothing touches you.

  • Turn on airplane mode first. The sensors and sound still work, and nothing is transmitted.
  • Calibration: rest one fingertip lightly on the rear camera and flash, and keep still.
  • Then place the phone 30-100 cm from your chest, bottom edge (speaker and mic) facing you, and lie still.
  • Keep the screen on. Phones often stop the microphone when the screen turns off.

Place the phone

  1. Put the phone on the bed or a bedside table, 30-100 cm from your chest.
  2. Point its bottom edge, where the speaker and mic are, at your chest.
  3. Lie still and breathe normally. Tracking starts after 8 seconds.
